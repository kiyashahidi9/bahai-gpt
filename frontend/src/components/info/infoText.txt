Welcome to BahaiGPT

I'm Kiya Shahidi, and I made this app because other AI models were giving me misinformed and inaccurate answers to my questions about my former religion, the Baha'i Faith. I hope that this app helps you on your search as it is with mine!

This model draws directly from the Writings themselves, and puts them above it's own words.

What can you do with it?
- Explain the Baha'i view on a concept
- Look for quotes on a concept
- Look for a specific quote
- History
- Choose random quotes

This model does NOT
- Create prayers or writings
- Misquote intentionally
- Respond well to "yo"

Enjoy!!! Thank you :))
