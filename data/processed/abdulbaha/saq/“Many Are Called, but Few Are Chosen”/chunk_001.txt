1Question: Christ says in the Gospel: “many are called, but few are chosen”, (From Matt. 22:14) and in the Qur’án it is written: “He singleth out for His mercy whomsoever He pleaseth.” (Qur’án 2:105 and 3:74) What is the wisdom of this?

2Answer: Know that the order and perfection of the universe require that existence should appear in countless forms. Created things cannot therefore be realized in a single degree, station, manner, kind, or species: Differences of degree, distinctions in form, and a multiplicity of kinds and species are inevitable.
