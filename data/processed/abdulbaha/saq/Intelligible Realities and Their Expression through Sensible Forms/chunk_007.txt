Exod. 13:21–2) Now, that which is intended is not a sensible form but an intelligible reality that has been expressed in such a form.

7Christ says, “The Father is in the Son, and the Son is in the Father.” (Cf. John 10:38) Now, was Christ within God or was God within Christ? No, by God! This is an intelligible condition which has been expressed in a sensible figure.

8We come to the explanation of the words of Bahá’u’lláh when He says: “O King! I was but a man like others, asleep upon My couch, when lo, the breezes of the All-Glorious were wafted over Me, and taught Me the knowledge of all that hath been.
