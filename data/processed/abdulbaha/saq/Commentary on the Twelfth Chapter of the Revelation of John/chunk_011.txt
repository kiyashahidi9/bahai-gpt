10“And the woman fled into the wilderness”; (From Rev. 12:6) that is, the religion of God betook itself to the desert, meaning the vast desert of Ḥijáz and the Arabian Peninsula.

11“Where she hath a place prepared of God”; (From Rev. 12:6) that is, the Arabian Peninsula became the home, the habitation, and the focal centre of the religion of God.

12“That they should feed her there a thousand two hundred and threescore days”. (From Rev. 12:6) According to the terminology of the Bible, these 1,260 days mean 1,260 years, as was before explained.
