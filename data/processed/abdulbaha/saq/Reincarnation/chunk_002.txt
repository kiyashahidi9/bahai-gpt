1Question: What is to be said about reincarnation, which is a belief upheld by the followers of certain religions?

2Answer: Our purpose in what we are about to say is to express the truth and not to denigrate the beliefs of others: It is merely to explain the facts of the matter and nothing more. Otherwise, we are neither inclined to dispute anyone’s deeply held beliefs, nor do we sanction such conduct.

3Know, then, that the reincarnationists are of two kinds. The first do not believe in spiritual rewards and punishments in the next world.
