1Question: Christ said: “I am the living bread which came down from heaven: if any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever.” (From John 6:51) What is the meaning of this utterance?

2Answer: By this bread is meant the heavenly sustenance of divine perfections. In other words, whoso partakes of this sustenance—that is, whoso acquires the outpouring grace of God, draws illumination from His light, and obtains his portion of the perfections of Christ—will attain everlasting life.
