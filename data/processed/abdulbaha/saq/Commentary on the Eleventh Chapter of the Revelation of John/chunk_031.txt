Now, even as Christ, you must lay down your lives in the path of the Beloved and suffer a martyr’s death. And so that Sun of Reality and that Moon of Guidance (The Báb and Quddús) both set, Christ-like, beneath the horizon of the supreme sacrifice and ascended to the realm of Heaven.

31“And their enemies beheld them.” (From Rev. 11:12) That is, many of their enemies realized after their martyrdom the sublimity of their station and the excellence of their virtues, and testified to their greatness and their perfections.

32“And the same hour was there a great earthquake, and the tenth part of the city fell, and in the earthquake were slain of men seven thousand.” (From Rev. 11:13) This earthquake occurred in Shíráz after the martyrdom of the Báb.
