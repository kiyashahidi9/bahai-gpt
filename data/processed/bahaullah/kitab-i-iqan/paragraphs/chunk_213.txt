All this generation could offer Us were wounds from its darts, and the only cup it proffered to Our lips was the cup of its venom. On Our neck We still bear the scar of chains, and upon Our body are imprinted the evidences of an unyielding cruelty.

And as to this man’s attainments, his ignorance, understanding and belief, behold what the Book which embraceth all things hath revealed; “Verily, the tree of Zaqqúm (Infernal tree) shall be the food of the Athím.” (Sinner or sinful. Qur’án 44:43–44) And then follow certain verses, until He saith: “Taste this, for thou forsooth art the mighty Karím!” (Honorable—Qur’án 44:49) Consider how clearly and explicitly he hath been described in God’s incorruptible Book! This man, moreover, feigning humility, hath in his own book referred to himself as the “athím servant”: “Athím” in the Book of God, mighty among the common herd, “Karím” in name!

Ponder the blessed verse, so that the meaning of the words “There is neither a thing green nor sere but it is noted in the unerring Book” (From Qur’án 6:59) may be imprinted upon the tablet of thy heart. Notwithstanding, a multitude bear him allegiance.
