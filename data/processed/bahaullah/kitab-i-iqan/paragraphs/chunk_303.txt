We conclude Our argument with His words—exalted is He—“And whoso shall withdraw from the remembrance of the Merciful, We will chain a Satan unto him, and he shall be his fast companion.” (From Qur’án 43:36) “And whoso turneth away from My remembrance, truly his shall be a life of misery.” (From Qur’án 20:124)

Thus hath it been revealed aforetime, were ye to comprehend.

Revealed by the “Bá’” and the “Há’.” (B and H meaning Bahá)
