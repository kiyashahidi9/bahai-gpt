3.3The first question: “In what tongue and towards what direction doth it behove us to worship the one true God?”

3.4The beginning of all utterance is the worship of God, and this followeth upon His recognition. Sanctified must be the eye if it is to truly recognize Him, and sanctified must be the tongue if it is to befittingly utter His praise.
