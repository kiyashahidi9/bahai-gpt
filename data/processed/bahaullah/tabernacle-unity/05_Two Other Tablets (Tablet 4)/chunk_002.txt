THE BEGINNING OF ALL UTTERANCE IS THE PRAISE OF GOD

4.1O servants! The springs of divine bestowal are streaming forth. Quaff ye therefrom, that by the aid of the incomparable Friend ye may be sanctified from this darksome world of dust and enter His abode. Renounce the world and direct your steps toward the city of the Beloved.

4.2O servants! The fire that consumeth all veils hath been kindled by My hand; quench it not with the waters of ignorance.
