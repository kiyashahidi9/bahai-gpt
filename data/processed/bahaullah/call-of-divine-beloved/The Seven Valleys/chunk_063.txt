61The wayfarer, after traversing the high planes of this supernal journey, entereth into the City of Contentment. In this valley he feeleth the breezes of divine contentment blowing from the plane of the spirit. He burneth away the veils of want, and with inward and outward eye perceiveth within and without all things the day of “God will satisfy everyone out of His abundance.” (From Qur’án 4:130) From sorrow he turneth to bliss, and from grief to joy, and from anguish and dejection to delight and rapture.
