49It is clear, then, that even these rays are not subject to egress or regress—how much less that Essence of existence and longed-for Mystery. O My brother, consider these matters in the spirit of enquiry, not in blind imitation. A true wayfarer will not be deterred by the impediment of words, nor daunted by the sway of insinuations.
How can a curtain part the lover from his love,
When Alexander’s wall cannot keep them apart? (Sa‘dí (ca. 1213–1292), author of the Gulistán and other poetical works)
