43O My brother! A pure heart is as a mirror; cleanse it with the burnish of love and severance from all save God, that the true sun may shine therein and the eternal morning dawn. Then wilt thou clearly see the meaning of “Earth and heaven cannot contain Me; what can alone contain Me is the heart of him that believeth in Me.” (From a Ḥadíth) And thou wilt take up thy life in thy hand and with infinite longing cast it before thy newly found Beloved.
