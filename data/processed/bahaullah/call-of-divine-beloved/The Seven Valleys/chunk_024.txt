22Wherefore must the veils of the satanic self be burned away in the fire of love, that the spirit may be cleansed and refined, and thus may apprehend the station of Him but for Whom the world would not have been created. (An allusion to the Ḥadíth in which God is said to address the Prophet Muhammad in these words: “But for Thee, I would not have created the spheres.”)
Kindle the fire of love and burn away all things;
Then set thy foot into the land of the lovers. (From a poem of Bahá’u’lláh)
