83In this city, even the veils of light are rent asunder and vanish away. “His beauty hath no veiling save light, His countenance no covering save revelation.” (From a Ḥadíth) How strange that the Beloved is as visible as the sun and yet the heedless still hunt after tinsel and base metal. Yea, the intensity of His revelation hath veiled Him, and the fullness of His shining forth hath hidden Him.
Even as the noontide sun
Hath the True One brightly shined,
But alas that He hath come
To the city of the blind! (Attributed to Rúmí)
