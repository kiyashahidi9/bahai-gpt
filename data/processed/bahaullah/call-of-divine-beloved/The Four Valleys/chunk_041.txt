40Astonishment here is highly prized, and utter poverty greatly cherished. Wherefore hath He said, “Poverty is My pride.” (From a Ḥadíth) And again: “God hath a people beneath the canopy of grandeur, whom He hath concealed in the garment of poverty to exalt in rank.” (From a poem cited in a number of Islamic sources) These are they who see with His eyes and hear with His ears, as hath been recorded in the well-known tradition. (See the Seven Valleys, ¶ 44)
