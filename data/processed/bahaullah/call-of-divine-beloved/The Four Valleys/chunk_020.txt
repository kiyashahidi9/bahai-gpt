19Wherefore, one must make ready the receptacle and become worthy of the descent of heavenly bestowals, that the all-sufficing Cup-Bearer may give one to drink of the wine of bounty from the crystal chalice of mercy. “For this let the striving strive!” (From Qur’án 83:26) And now do I say, “Verily, we are God’s, and to Him shall we return.” (From Qur’án 2:156)
