Erelong will God consume their souls in the fire. He, verily, is the fiercest of avengers.

12The people surrounded the house, and Muslims and Christians wept over Us, and the voice of lamentation was upraised between earth and heaven by reason of what the hands of the oppressors had wrought. We perceived that the weeping of the people of the Son exceeded the weeping of others—a sign for such as ponder.

13One of My companions offered up his life, cutting his throat with his own hands for the love of God, an act unheard of in bygone centuries and which God hath set apart for this Revelation as an evidence of the power of His might. (Ḥájí Ja‘far-i-Tabrízí; he was prevented in time from ending his life) He, verily, is the Unconstrained, the All-Subduing.
