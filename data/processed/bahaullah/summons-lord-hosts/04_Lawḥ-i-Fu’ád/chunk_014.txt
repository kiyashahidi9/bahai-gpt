12Then an angel from the right hand of the Throne summoned him: “Behold the angel of affliction. Is there any place to flee to save hell, wherein the heart (“Heart” translates Fu’ád, the given name of the Ottoman minister) boileth?” And the angel of chastisement received his spirit, and a voice proclaimed: “Enter the bottomless pit which hath been promised in the Book, and whose existence thou didst day and night deny!”

13Soon will We dismiss the one who was like unto him, (‘Álí Páshá) and will lay hold on their Chief who ruleth the land, (Sulṭán ‘Abdu’l-‘Azíz) and I, verily, am the Almighty, the All-Compelling. Be thou steadfast in the Cause of God and extol thy Lord morn and eve.
